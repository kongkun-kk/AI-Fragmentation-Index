Legal texts themselves are official/public documents of their respective
jurisdictions. This repository stores links and metadata for those texts, not
the texts themselves, wherever redistribution is unclear.

Person-level talent-flow data are never stored in this repository; only
aggregated measures and synthetic data will be published.
